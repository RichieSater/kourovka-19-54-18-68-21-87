# Kourovka finite-group research program

This repository is the umbrella workspace for three finite-group problems from
the 21st Kourovka Notebook:

| Work order | Problem | Short name | Current state |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | 19.54 | chief factors under the strictly-2-maximal condition | **not started** |
| 2 | 18.68 | composition factors with complemented maximals | **not started** |
| 3 | 21.87 | generation from subgroups of coprime-index gcd | **not started** |

The repository currently contains **research scaffolding and an attack order
only**. It does not claim a new theorem, a classification, a counterexample, or
a solution to any of the three problems.

## Canonical statements

- **19.54.** What are the chief factors of a finite group in which every
  2-maximal subgroup is not \(m\)-maximal for any \(m\geq 3\)?
- **18.68.** What are the nonabelian composition factors of a finite
  nonsoluble group all of whose maximal subgroups have complements?
- **21.87.** Assume that a finite group \(G\) has a family of
  \(d\)-generator subgroups whose indices have no common divisor. Is it true
  that \(G\) can be generated by \(d+1\) elements?

These transcriptions were checked against the July 2026 edition of the 21st
Kourovka Notebook. Exact provenance is recorded in
[`docs/SOURCE-LEDGER.md`](docs/SOURCE-LEDGER.md).

## Layout

```text
problem-19-54/       strictly 2-maximal subgroups and chief factors
problem-18-68/       complemented maximal subgroups and composition factors
problem-21-87/       coprime-index families and generator bounds
shared/              infrastructure that is genuinely property-independent
docs/                provenance, prior-work audit, and research protocol
scripts/             repository-level validation only
```

Each problem owns its own `gap/`, `tests/`, `data/`, `notes/`, and
`references/` directories. This separation is deliberate: quotient
inheritance, maximal-subgroup tests, and certificate meanings differ between
the three properties and must not be silently conflated.
