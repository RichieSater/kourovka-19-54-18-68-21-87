# Initial literature search — 2026-08-11

## Bottom line

The July 2026 21st Kourovka Notebook still prints all three questions in open
problem form and records the same foundational partial results used in this
scaffold. A targeted ten-query search found the core papers listed below and
did not surface a later paper explicitly claiming to solve Problems 19.54,
18.68, or 21.87. That is a reconnaissance result, **not** a proof of novelty
or openness; each project must repeat a dedicated citation and author search
before making either claim.

## Findings by problem

### 19.54

- Monakhov--Sokhor explicitly reformulate the Notebook condition as equality
  of the 2-maximal and strictly-2-maximal classes and list soluble and simple
  examples.
- Meng--Guo give a necessary-and-sufficient condition in the soluble case:
  every non-Frattini chief factor is strongly irreducible as a module.
- The search found later work on strictly 2-maximal subgroups, but no source in
  the returned results advertised the requested general chief-factor
  classification.

### 18.68

- The official Notebook records the simple-group list
  \(L_2(7),L_2(11),L_5(2)\).
- It separately records the corresponding composition-factor result for the
  stronger Hall-maximal hypothesis and that the Hall-maximal hypothesis
  implies complementability.
- This distinction is essential: the desired converse reduction for arbitrary
  groups is not supplied by those statements.

### 21.87

- The official Notebook records the soluble \(d+1\) theorem of Kovacs--Sim
  and the arbitrary \(d+2\) theorem of Lucchini.
- The publisher record for Lucchini's paper was found.
- No returned result advertised closure of the one-generator gap.
