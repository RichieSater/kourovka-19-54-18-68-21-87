# Problem-specific review overlays

The generic workflow sets the burden of proof. These overlays identify the
highest-risk obligations for each open problem.

## Problem 19.54

- **Initial:** exact equivalence of 2-maximal, weak-second-maximal, and strict
  terminology; soluble theorem reconstruction; full chief-factor claim.
- **Round 1:** quotient/inheritance audit and validity of any monolithic
  reduction.
- **Round 2:** all maximal overgroups and subgroup-lattice completeness for
  simple/almost-simple families.
- **Round 3:** embedding-aware GAP certificates, positive-coverage receipts,
  and mutation tests for omitted overgroups.
- **Round 4:** synthesis of Frattini/non-Frattini and abelian/nonabelian chief
  factors without silently classifying only the simple case.

## Problem 18.68

- **Initial:** complement convention, known simple baseline, and a section-safe
  composition-factor theorem.
- **Round 1:** quotient, extension, and direct-product behavior of `CMP`.
- **Round 2:** primitive/monolithic reduction, split versus nonsplit
  extensions, and exact use of cohomology.
- **Round 3:** exhaustive complement witnesses/failures and bounded primitive
  scans with explicit unknown states.
- **Round 4:** proof that arbitrary composition factors, not merely socle
  factors, lie in the final list.

## Problem 21.87

- **Initial:** reconstruction of Kovacs--Sim and Lucchini and identification of
  the exact extra-generator step.
- **Round 1:** gcd/Sylow reformulation, quotient behavior, and preservation of
  the same value of \(d\).
- **Round 2:** primitive and crown-based-power completeness, including all
  nonabelian-socle cases.
- **Round 3:** certified subgroup families, independent \(d(G)\) lower bounds,
  and searches that distinguish absence from incomplete enumeration.
- **Round 4:** close the exact \(d+1\) statement; prevent a proof of only a
  nearby bound or only fixed small \(d\).
