## Main theorem

If a finite group \(G\) has a family \(\mathcal H\) with
\(d(H)\leq d\) for all \(H\in\mathcal H\) and

\[
  \gcd\{\lvert G:H\rvert:H\in\mathcal H\}=1,
\]

then \(d(G)\leq d+1\).

## Dependency graph

1. Quotient inheritance and prime-by-prime Sylow coverage are proved
   directly.
2. Lucchini's augmentation-ideal theorem gives
   \(d_G(I_G)\leq d+1\).
3. A least counterexample has positive presentation rank. Gruenberg's
   theorem then excludes nontrivial soluble normal subgroups.
4. Dalla Volta--Lucchini reduce the group to a critical crown-based power
   \(L_k\) with nonabelian socle \(A\), with an exact formula for \(k\).
5. Detomi--Lucchini give the lower estimate
   \(k\geq1+|A|^{d+1}/(2|\Gamma_{L,A}|)\).
6. Guralnick's generation theorem gives
   \(\lvert C_{\operatorname{Aut}(S)}(P)\rvert\leq |S|/2\), and hence the
   corresponding monolithic bound.
7. The \(d\)-generated subgroup containing a Sylow \(2\)-subgroup yields
   \(k\) distinct automorphism orbits in a fiber of size \(|A|^d\), giving
   \(k\leq|A|^{d+1}/(2|\Gamma_{L,A}|)\), a contradiction.

## Structural reductions

The proof keeps the same parameter \(d\) in every quotient. It does not infer
the target hypothesis for arbitrary subgroups, sections, socles, or
extensions. The abelian-socle case is excluded only after positive
presentation rank and Gruenberg's soluble-normal-subgroup theorem have been
applied.
