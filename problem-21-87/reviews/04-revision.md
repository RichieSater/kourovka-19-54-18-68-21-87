## Theorem consistency audit

The audit starts from the fixed statement for every integer \(d\geq0\) and
tracks the same parameter through quotient inheritance, the augmentation
ideal, the critical crown with \(m=d+1\), and the final orbit packing.  The
separate \(d=0\) argument and the \(m\geq2\) hypothesis for the crown theorem
meet exactly.  The monolithic base is a proper quotient before the
Detomi--Lucchini bound is invoked, so \(m\geq d(L)\).  The lower bound contains
the strict extra \(1\), while the upper bound has the same denominator and
exponent because \(m=d+1\).

The pointwise-centralizer chain was checked in both stages: restriction from
\(\Gamma_{L,A}\) to \(\operatorname{Aut}(A)\) is injective, fixing the product
Sylow subgroup prevents factor permutations, and Guralnick's involution gives
a free orbit in an involution class.  In the final coordinate count,
\(T\leq H\) supplies the supported socle element and puts the projected Sylow
subgroup inside the generated coordinate subgroup.  No property is inferred
for an unproved section, extension, or arbitrary subgroup.
