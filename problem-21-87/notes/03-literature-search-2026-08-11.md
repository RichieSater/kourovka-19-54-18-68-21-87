# Dedicated literature and priority search — 2026-08-11

**Classification:** search reconnaissance. It supports no claim of novelty or
continued openness.

## Scope and method

The search targeted the exact problem, the two papers named by the Notebook,
the later crown and probabilistic-generation literature, and forward
citations. Ten deliberately varied web queries were executed, followed by
primary-source retrieval and citation-graph checks in Google Scholar and
OpenAlex. Publisher, official repository, Numdam, Cambridge, author-archive,
and arXiv records were preferred to aggregators.

## Ten queries

1. “Kourovka” “21.87”
2. “Problem 21.87” finite group generators
3. “d-generator subgroups” “coprime index” d+1
4. “d-generator subgroups of coprime index” solution
5. “On groups with d-generator subgroups of coprime index” citations
6. Lucchini coprime index d+1 finite group crown
7. finite group subgroups indices no common divisor d+1 generators
8. critical crown based power conditional generation probability 1/2
9. Detomi Lucchini unique minimal normal subgroup P_L_N 1/2
10. Sylow 2-subgroup involution automorphism centralizer finite simple group

Targeted follow-ups searched the exact titles of Lucchini's 2000 paper,
Dalla Volta--Lucchini 1998, Detomi--Lucchini 2013, and Guralnick 1986, with
PDF, theorem phrases, author names, and DOI variants.

## Findings

1. The July 2026 21st Kourovka Notebook still prints Problem 21.87 as a
   question. It records Kovacs--Sim's soluble \(d+1\) theorem and Lucchini's
   arbitrary \(d+2\) theorem.
2. Full text was recovered for Kovacs--Sim 1991, Lucchini 1990, Lucchini
   1992, Dalla Volta--Lucchini 1998, and Lucchini--Menegazzo 1997. The proof
   in the present manuscript does not use the inaccessible proof of
   Lucchini 2000.
3. The official University of Padua record and the publisher abstract for
   Detomi--Lucchini 2013 state that
   \(P_{L,N}(m)\geq1/2\) for every \(m\geq d(L)\). Detomi's public 2013
   conference slides state the stronger \(53/90\) bound when the socle is
   nonabelian.
4. Burness--Guralnick explicitly record Guralnick's 1986 theorem that every
   finite nonabelian simple group is generated by a Sylow \(2\)-subgroup and
   an involution.
5. Google Scholar displayed eight citing items for Lucchini 2000 during the
   manual citation check. None of their titles or available abstracts
   claimed the \(d+1\) conclusion.
6. OpenAlex listed five citing works for Lucchini 2000: two papers on free
   profinite products, the 2001 minimal-generation probability paper,
   “Boundedly generated subgroups of finite groups”, and a paper on free
   pro-\(\mathcal S\)-groups. No listed work asserted a solution of Problem
   21.87.
7. OpenAlex listed seventeen retrievable records citing Detomi--Lucchini
   2013. Their titles concern probabilistic generation, invariable
   generation, subgroup generation, generating graphs, crowns, and
   algorithms; none advertised this problem's conclusion.

## Limits

- No MathSciNet or zbMATH subscription search was performed.
- The full Detomi--Lucchini publisher PDF and the full Lucchini 2000 article
  were not openly accessible. Exact acceptance-critical statements were
  checked in official abstracts/records and, for Detomi--Lucchini, public
  author conference slides.
- Author correspondence was not attempted.
- Search-engine and citation-index coverage is incomplete and changes over
  time.

## Safe conclusion

The search found no prior source claiming the affirmative solution proved in
the manuscript. This is only a dated negative search result. The paper and
repository therefore avoid claims of novelty, priority, or definitively
settled publication status until specialist review and a broader bibliographic
check are completed.
