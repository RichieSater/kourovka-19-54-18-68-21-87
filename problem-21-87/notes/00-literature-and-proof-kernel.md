## Exact parameter convention

Throughout this work, a *\(d\)-generator subgroup* means a subgroup generated
by **at most** \(d\) elements. This is the convention used in the statements
and proofs of Kovacs--Sim (1991, Theorem 2) and Lucchini (2000).

## Elementary reductions

Let \(\mathcal H\) be a family of subgroups of a finite group \(G\).

1. **Proved.** A finite subfamily has the same gcd of indices. Indeed the gcd
   of any family of positive divisors of \(|G|\) is attained after successively
   adjoining finitely many members, since a strictly decreasing chain of
   positive divisors terminates.
2. **Proved.** The condition
   \(\gcd\{\lvert G:H\rvert:H\in\mathcal H\}=1\) is equivalent to: for every prime
   \(p\mid |G|\), some \(H\in\mathcal H\) has \(p\nmid\lvert G:H\rvert\). This is just
   the prime-divisor characterization of a gcd.
3. **Proved.** For such an \(H\), a Sylow \(p\)-subgroup of \(H\) is also a
   Sylow \(p\)-subgroup of \(G\); equivalently, \(H\) contains a Sylow
   \(p\)-subgroup of \(G\).
4. **Proved.** The hypothesis passes to every quotient \(G/N\) with the same
   value of \(d\): the images \(HN/N\) remain at most \(d\)-generated and
   \(\lvert G/N:HN/N\rvert=\lvert G:HN\rvert\) divides \(\lvert G:H\rvert\), so the gcd of the image indices
   divides 1.
5. **Proved.** The family is an invariable generating family of subgroups:
   for arbitrary choices \(x_H\in G\),
   \(\langle H^{x_H}:H\in\mathcal H\rangle=G\). Its index divides every
   \(\lvert G:H\rvert\), and hence divides their gcd.

## Published baseline recovered in full

- Kovacs--Sim, *Generating finite soluble groups*, Indag. Math. 2 (1991),
  Theorem 2, proves \(d(G)\le d+1\) for soluble \(G\). Its proof uses crowns
  of irreducible modules.
- Lucchini, *Generating the augmentation ideal*, Rend. Sem. Mat. Univ.
  Padova 88 (1992), Theorem 2 and Proposition 2, proves
  \(d_G(I_G)\le d+1\) for arbitrary finite \(G\). Together with
  \(d(G)=d_G(I_G)+\operatorname{pr}(G)\), this gives the desired bound for
  groups of zero presentation rank.
- Lucchini, *On groups with \(d\)-generator subgroups of coprime index*,
  Comm. Algebra 28 (2000), proves the unconditional \(d+2\) bound.
- Dalla Volta--Lucchini's crown-based-power results and later conditional
  generation formulas identify a nonabelian crown as the only possible
  source of the final one-generator gap.

The complete 2000 article is not openly redistributed by the publisher. A
free first page and full bibliographic record were recovered, while the proof
is being reconstructed from the cited primary inputs; no claim will depend on
a search snippet.

## Modern input closing the crown count

**Published input.** Detomi--Lucchini (2013) prove that the conditional
generation probability of a monolithic group satisfies
\(P_{L,A}(m)\geq 1/2\) whenever \(m\geq d(L)\). Combined with the exact
critical-crown formula of Dalla Volta--Lucchini (1998), this closes the
one-generator gap. The complete argument is in
[`02-crown-counting-proof.md`](02-crown-counting-proof.md).

**Published CFSG input.** Guralnick (1986) proves that every finite
nonabelian simple group is generated by a Sylow \(2\)-subgroup and an
involution. It yields the pointwise-centralizer estimate needed in the orbit
packing argument.
