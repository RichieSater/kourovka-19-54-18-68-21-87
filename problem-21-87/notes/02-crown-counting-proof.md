# Crown-counting proof map

**Status:** **proved modulo published inputs**. The canonical complete proof is
[paper/main.tex](../paper/main.tex). This note isolates the argument for
auditing; it is not a substitute for the manuscript.

## Result

Let \(G\) be finite. If \(G\) has a family \(\mathcal H\) of subgroups with
\(d(H)\leq d\) for every \(H\in\mathcal H\) and

\[
  \gcd\{\lvert G:H\rvert:H\in\mathcal H\}=1,
\]

then \(d(G)\leq d+1\).

## Published inputs

1. **Lucchini 1992, Theorem 2 and Proposition 2.** The index hypothesis
   gives \(d_G(I_G)\leq d+1\), where \(I_G\) is the augmentation ideal.
2. **Gruenberg 1976, p. 218; Lucchini 1990, 2.1, p. 209.** If
   \(\operatorname{pr}(X)>0\) and \(N\unlhd X\) is soluble, then
   \(d(X)=d(X/N)\).
3. **Dalla Volta--Lucchini 1998, Theorems 1.4 and 2.7.** A finite group
   which is not \(m\)-generated although all its proper quotients are is a
   critical crown-based power \(L_k\). For nonabelian socle \(A\),
   \[
     k=1+\frac{P_{L,A}(m)|A|^m}{|\Gamma_{L,A}|},
   \]
   where \(\Gamma_{L,A}\) consists of the automorphisms of \(L\) inducing the
   identity on \(L/A\).
4. **Detomi--Lucchini 2013.** If \(L\) is monolithic with socle \(A\) and
   \(m\geq d(L)\), then \(P_{L,A}(m)\geq 1/2\).
5. **Guralnick 1986.** Every finite nonabelian simple group is generated by
   a Sylow \(2\)-subgroup and an involution. Burness--Guralnick 2024 states
   this form explicitly in its introduction.

Inputs 3--5 depend on the classification of finite simple groups.

## Elementary reductions

- **Quotients.** The image \(HN/N\) is at most \(d\)-generated, and
  \(\lvert G/N:HN/N\rvert=\lvert G:HN\rvert\) divides
  \(\lvert G:H\rvert\). Thus the hypothesis passes to \(G/N\) with the same
  \(d\).
- **Sylow coverage.** For every prime \(p\mid |G|\), some \(H\in\mathcal H\)
  has \(p\nmid\lvert G:H\rvert\), and hence contains a Sylow \(p\)-subgroup
  of \(G\).
- **Finite subfamily.** A decreasing chain of gcds of divisors of
  \(|G|\) stabilizes, so an arbitrary qualifying family may be replaced by a
  finite one.

## Centralizer estimates

Let \(S\) be finite nonabelian simple and \(P\in\operatorname{Syl}_2(S)\).
Choose an involution \(t\) with \(S=\langle P,t\rangle\). If
\(D=C_{\operatorname{Aut}(S)}(P)\), then the \(S\)-class \(t^S\) is
\(D\)-invariant. The stabilizer \(D_t\) is trivial, because it fixes \(P\)
pointwise and fixes \(t\). Therefore

\[
 |D|\leq |t^S|=\lvert S:C_S(t)\rvert\leq |S|/2.       \tag{1}
\]

Now let \(L\) be monolithic with nonabelian socle
\(A=S_1\times\cdots\times S_n\), and let
\(R\in\operatorname{Syl}_2(L)\). Restriction embeds
\(\Gamma_{L,A}\) in \(\operatorname{Aut}(A)\). An automorphism fixing
\(R\cap A=P_1\times\cdots\times P_n\) pointwise fixes every direct factor
and restricts to an element of \(C_{\operatorname{Aut}(S_i)}(P_i)\).
Consequently,

\[
 |C_{\Gamma_{L,A}}(R)|
 \leq (|S_1|/2)^n\leq |A|/2.                           \tag{2}
\]

## Minimal-counterexample count

Assume a counterexample of least order and put \(m=d+1\). Every proper
quotient is \(m\)-generated. Lucchini's augmentation bound and
\(d(G)=d_G(I_G)+\operatorname{pr}(G)\) show that
\(\operatorname{pr}(G)>0\). Gruenberg's result then excludes every
nontrivial soluble normal subgroup.

The critical-crown theorem gives \(G\cong L_k\). Its socle \(A\) is
nonabelian, and \(L\) is a proper quotient, so \(d(L)\leq m\). The exact
formula and the Detomi--Lucchini bound give

\[
 k\geq 1+\frac{|A|^m}{2|\Gamma_{L,A}|}.                \tag{3}
\]

Choose an at-most-\(d\)-generated \(H\leq G\) containing a Sylow
\(2\)-subgroup \(T\) of \(G\), and write

\[
 H=\langle h_1,\ldots,h_d\rangle,
 \qquad h_i=(h_{i1},\ldots,h_{ik})\in L_k.
\]

The coordinate tuples
\(\mathbf h_j=(h_{1j},\ldots,h_{dj})\) lie in one fiber
\(\Omega\subseteq L^d\) of size \(|A|^d\). They belong to distinct
\(\Gamma_{L,A}\)-orbits: a relation between coordinates \(r\neq s\) would
hold for every element of \(H\), whereas \(T\cap A^k\) contains a
nonidentity element supported only at \(r\).

The stabilizer of \(\mathbf h_j\) fixes the \(j\)-th image of \(H\)
pointwise. That image contains the Sylow \(2\)-subgroup obtained by
projecting \(T\). By (2), every orbit has size at least
\(2|\Gamma_{L,A}|/|A|\). Packing the \(k\) distinct orbits into \(\Omega\)
gives

\[
 k\leq\frac{|A|^{d+1}}{2|\Gamma_{L,A}|}
   =\frac{|A|^m}{2|\Gamma_{L,A}|},                     \tag{4}
\]

contradicting (3). The case \(d=0\) is immediate.

## Sharp step

The lower and upper counts have the same factor \(1/2\). The extra \(1\) in
the exact critical-crown formula makes them incompatible. This is the step
that removes the previously published extra generator.
