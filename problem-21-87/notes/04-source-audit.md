The text layer of the Dalla Volta--Lucchini PDF drops the horizontal bar from
some inequality symbols.  The hypotheses (m\geq2) in Theorem 1.4 and
(m\geq d(L)) in Theorem 2.7 were therefore checked visually on printed
pp. 85 and 87, rather than inferred from OCR.  This includes the endpoint
(m=2), needed when the problem parameter is (d=1).

## Source-access boundary

The full Detomi--Lucchini 2013 version of record was access-restricted. Its
official abstract states exactly the inequality used, and the public
conference slides state a stronger result in the nonabelian-socle case
actually reached by the proof. The manuscript treats the result as a
published input and does not claim an independent audit of its CFSG casework.

The full Lucchini 2000 article was also not openly accessible. It is cited
only for the historical \(d+2\) baseline already recorded by the current
Notebook; no step of the new proof depends on its proof.

The full Roggenkamp 1979 chapter was access-restricted.  The official Springer
record confirms the chapter identity, pages, and DOI.  Lucchini 1992, p. 146,
explicitly attributes the displayed presentation-rank identity to
Roggenkamp's chapter, and later literature identifies it as Theorem 2.1.

## Interpretation checks

- A “\(d\)-generator subgroup” is generated by at most \(d\) elements,
  matching the primary proofs. Padding by identity elements converts a
  shorter tuple to length \(d\).
- All subgroup indices use the modern finite-group convention
  \(\lvert G:H\rvert\), never square delimiters.
- \(C_{\operatorname{Aut}(X)}(Y)\) means pointwise centralizer in the
  manuscript; this is stated before the centralizer lemmas.
- The proof uses no claim about inheritance by sections, subgroups, socles,
  or extensions beyond the lemmas proved in the manuscript.
